<h1 align="center">Hi! I am David Cuevas Alba</h1>

<h3 align="left">👩‍💻 About Me</h3>

I am a Systems Engineer and Graduate Assistant in Robotics at Universidad de los Andes, currently pursuing my Master's in Systems and Computer Engineering (MISIS). I am deeply passionate about advancing the integration of robotics in society and bridging the technological gap in Latin America through cutting-edge AI solutions.

As a member and former leader of [SinfonIA Uniandes](https://sinfoniauniandes.github.io/SinfonIA-web/home), I helped lead our team to victory at the RoboCup@Home Social Standard Platform League 2024.

* 🔭 **Currently Focusing On:** Embodied Agentic AI, Vision-Language-Action models (VLAs), and Action Chunking with Transformers (ACTs).
* 📚 **Research Interests:** Social robotics, NLP (summarization, RAG), and Computer Vision (YOLO).
* ⚡ **Hardware Experience:** Unitree G1, SoftBank Robotics Pepper, NAO, and P3DX.

<h3 align="left">🔬 Research Lines & Development</h3>

* **Robotics:** Designing intelligent behaviors for robots in social environments, utilizing ROS 1 and ROS 2 Jazzy for perception, STT, TTS, and autonomous navigation.
* **Mobile Interfaces:** Developing native Android and WearOS applications to serve as control and monitoring interfaces for robotic systems, leveraging libraries like jros2 built directly on a Fast DDS C++ backend.
* **XR Development:** Building experiences with Unity, OpenXR, Meta SDK, and C#.

### 📂 Featured Projects and Repositories

- **[jros2 Cellphone Interface](https://github.com/SinfonIAUniandes/jros2_cellphone_interface)** *Category:* Mobile & Robotics 📱🤖 | *Tags:* ROS 2, Android, Fast DDS, C++  
  Native Android interface transforming a smartphone into a dual-joystick controller for robotic systems. To ensure high-performance, low-latency communication, this library is built directly on a Fast DDS C++ backend rather than relying on traditional abstraction layers.

- **[RoboCup@Home - Task Utilities](https://github.com/SinfonIAUniandes/task_utilities)** *Category:* Robotics 🤖 | *Tags:* ROS, Python, Social Robotics  
  Core task execution systems and state machines developed for our winning entry at the RoboCup@Home Social Standard Platform League 2024.

- **[LLM Robot Agents](https://github.com/dcuevasa/LLM-Robot-Agents)** *Category:* AI & Robotics 🧠🤖 | *Tags:* LLMs, Autonomous Agents, Embodied AI  
  Frameworks and architectures for evaluating and deploying Large Language Model-based autonomous agents for complex task execution with social robots.

- **[LeRobot SO101 MuJoCo Simulation](https://github.com/SinfonIAUniandes/lerobot_robot_so101_mujoco)** *Category:* Simulation & AI 🦾 | *Tags:* MuJoCo, ACT, VLA, LeRobot  
  High-fidelity MuJoCo simulation environment for the SO101 robotic arm, utilized for fine-tuning Vision-Language-Action (VLA) models like SmolVLA and Action Chunking with Transformers (ACTs).

- **[SoftBank Robots VR Teleoperation](https://github.com/Oasis-Uniandes/softbank_robots_vr_teleop)** *Category:* XR & Robotics 🥽 | *Tags:* Unity 6, OpenXR, Meta Quest 3, ROS  
  Immersive virtual reality teleoperation interface bridging spatial computing with ROS to control SoftBank robotic platforms.

- **[WeaROS2](https://github.com/SinfonIAUniandes/WeaROS2)** *Category:* Mobile ⌚ | *Tags:* WearOS, ROS 2, Mobile Sensors  
  Smartwatch application designed as a wearable control and monitoring interface, seamlessly bridging smartwatch sensor data with ROS 2 ecosystems.

- **[Pepper NLP & Vision Utilities](https://github.com/SinfonIAUniandes/speech_utilities)** *Category:* AI 🧠 | *Tags:* NLP, YOLO, Python, Real-time  
  Comprehensive perception systems for the Pepper robot, combining real-time speech recognition, natural language processing, and YOLO-based object detection.
